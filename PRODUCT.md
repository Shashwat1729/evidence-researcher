# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Primary:** Independent researchers, journalists, documentary makers, and serious students investigating complex historical, scientific, or cultural questions where evidence quality matters more than speed. They work under low ambient light (evening research), need traceability for publication, and distrust generic chatbot answers.
- **Secondary:** Product researchers and analysts comparing hypotheses (e.g., economic vs military explanations) who need competing views surfaced, not hidden.
- **Context:** Solo, focused work at a desktop. The job is "find the best available evidence, understand what it actually establishes, and report uncertainty honestly" — not "get a fast answer."

## Product Purpose

Evidence-first deep research agent powered by Gemini (reasoning engine, not the product). The product orchestrates the full research loop — plan → diverse search → retrieval → claim extraction → contradiction hunting → provenance → synthesis — and exposes every step for audit. Success is when another researcher can trace every conclusion back to its source and see what could not be established.

## Brand Personality

Rigorous, calm, trustworthy. Three words: **measured · transparent · authoritative**.

Voice: precise, unadorned, confident without hype. No marketing superlatives, no anthropomorphism, no performative cleverness. The interface should feel like a well-kept archive: quiet, ordered, and serious about evidence.

## Anti-references

- **Generic chatbot chrome** (floating bubbles, gradient hero, "Ask me anything" emptiness) — this is a research instrument, not a chat toy.
- **SaaS marketing tropes** (metric hero, identical card grids, tiny uppercase eyebrows on every section) — they trivialize a tool that must feel credible.
- **AI slop palettes** — cream/sand/parchment body, neon gradients, glassmorphism, sketchy SVG illustrations, 32px+ rounded cards with soft shadows. All read as "generated."
- **Side-stripe accents and ghost cards** (1px border + 16px+ shadow) — decorative, not meaningful.

## Design Principles

1. **Show, don't tell.** Every claim links to its passage and source; provenance is visual, not asserted.
2. **Expert confidence.** Hierarchy and typography do the work — weight and size, not color or decoration, signal importance.
3. **Traceability over brevity.** Searched vs found vs verified vs uncertain are distinct states; never collapse them for neatness.
4. **Calm under load.** Long runs expose progress without noise; skeletons, not spinners, hold space.
5. **Respect the evidence.** Tier and access are explained, not gamified; uncertainty is first-class content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 body text, keyboard-navigable tabs, ARIA live regions for progress, reduced-motion alternatives). Tested with keyboard-only and 200% zoom. Color is never the sole signal for tier or claim state.
